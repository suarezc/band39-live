Safe Card: the money side of the care app

How I plan to build the financial screens. Lisa gets a Money tab in her app; Margaret's kitchen screen gets two calm states. Every name, amount and shop below is made up. The look matches the care app in care/web.

1. Lisa's app · the Money tab

One place for her mother's card: what it allows, what happened, and anything waiting for her.

2. The care shop · paying with a one-time card

A suggestion from the band's data, paid with a card that exists for that one purchase.

3. Margaret's screen · kitchen tablet, large type

She never sees limits, declines or the word "scam". Two states only.

4. How the pieces connect

From what the band hears to the card, in order.

1Band hears speechMicrophone → Deepgram text, per speaker.
2Whose voice?Voice match + loudness: Mom, a known person, or unknown.
3Scam wording?Decision model on the unknown voice's words.
4FreezeUnknown voice + scam wording → the card stops.
5Lisa decidesCall, unfreeze, or keep frozen. Only a person unfreezes.
