          How the data gets to the band, and back

          Five steps: sense, transfer, remember, act, share. The band is small and simple: it listens, senses movement and heart rate, and pulses. The phone does the heavy lifting, and the cloud keeps the memory. Protocol details are illustrative until confirmed with the Vexo SDK.

          1
          ⌚
          Sense

          The band’s microphone hears speech, and motion, heart-rate, and skin-temperature sensors watch activity and rest.

          ➜
          2
          📶
          Transfer

          Data goes to the phone over encrypted Bluetooth LE. Audio is sent in short compressed chunks only when someone is speaking. Sensors send a summary batch about every minute.

          ➜
          3
          🧠
          Remember

          The phone transcribes speech and deletes the raw audio. The cloud pulls out people, meds, appointments, and promises, each with its source quote.

          ➜
          4
          ⚡
          Act

          The agent follows autonomy levels: remember, remind, act-and-tell, ask first, escalate. It acts through Calendar, Email, Notion, and SMS.

          ➜
          5
          💌
          Share & respond

          Family gets summaries and alerts. Back on the band, haptic pulses talk to Margaret and the earbud speaks.

          Information flows both ways, on Margaret’s terms

          Most buyers are adult children like Lisa: working, often far away, and worried. Vexo gives them peace of mind without turning Mom into someone under surveillance.

          📋
          Daily digest

          One calm summary each evening: meds, appointments, conversations, activity, and anything worth knowing.

          🛡️
          Real-time alerts

          Only for things that matter right away: a scam blocked, a possible fall with no response, the door opening at night.

          💌
          Two-way messages

          Mom says “tell Lisa…” and taps twice. Lisa’s reply arrives as a pulse and is read aloud. “I’m visiting Saturday” becomes a reminder.

          📓
          Shared care binder

          Visit summaries, medication list, and upcoming appointments in one Notion page any family member can open.

          📖
          Memory book

          Stories Mom tells, saved with her permission, become something the family keeps.

          🔐
          Sharing tiers

          The wearer controls what is shared: summaries, not transcripts. Location only in emergencies or when she’s off-route.

          Built for dignity, consent, and trust

          🎙 Transcribe, then discard

          Raw audio never leaves the phone and is deleted after transcription. A visible listening light and one-tap pause.

          🤝 Consent for everyone

          Visit mode asks clinicians for consent. Onboarding includes the wearer and her healthcare proxy.

          🔎 No invented memories

          Every answer cites a source quote. When unsure, Vexo says “I’m not sure” instead of filling the gap.

          🩺 Not a diagnosis

          Vexo notices changes and suggests a check-in. It never claims to detect dementia (the FDA general-wellness boundary).

          🔐 Wearer-controlled sharing

          Family sees summaries and flags, never transcripts. Sharing tiers are set together.

          🎯 Few false alarms

          Confidence tiers, and the wearer confirms before escalation, except in high-confidence emergencies.
